# Code of conduct

proto-kit follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, critique work and not people, and make
room for designers and developers of every level. Harassment, insults and
personal attacks are not accepted in issues, pull requests or any project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainers
privately through the contact details on the repository owner's GitHub
profile. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, close
discussions or block people who break this code.
